# Assemble a weekly note

Use the occurrence date to select fragments. Suppose a corrected export was delivered on Friday, January 3, and the worker writes it up on Monday, January 6. The delivery belongs to Friday's week. If Monday's work was a new analysis, the analysis has its own Monday fragment.

Within the week, group fragments by subject and retain the writer and evidence reference until editing is complete. When an author adds information to the same subject and day, read the existing fragment and append the new evidence. Different authors keep separate files. If two fragments point to the same delivery, verify the evidence and mention the delivery once.

During editing, check three things. Can the reader tell what they can use now? Is the scope of the result clear? Can they distinguish a workaround for a particular input from a repair that works on new inputs? Have a person approve the wording before sharing the report.

In the [worked weekly note](../examples/weekly.md), the reader learns that the corrected batch can be used. The [sweep evidence](../examples/sweep-first.md) totals **3 records, 2 direct sessions, 1 relay**. Those records describe the investigation and do not represent three deliveries.
